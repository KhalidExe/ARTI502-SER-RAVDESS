| D-01 | 2026-11-21 | detail | **Augmentation is precomputed + on the fly.** For every utterance, 4 waveform-augmented copies are computed once (time stretch 0.9–1.1 p=0.5, pitch shift ±2 semitones p=0.5, gain ±6 dB p=0.5, Gaussian noise SNR 15–40 dB p=0.5; fixed seed 1234). SpecAugment (2 freq masks ≤12.5 % of bands, 2 time masks ≤20 frames and ≤10 % of length) is applied on the fly to every training sample. Validation/test always use clean features. **Approval conditions (implemented):** (1) augmented copies inherit the actor ID of their source utterance; the training Dataset asserts at construction that it only serves actors of the round's training folds, every run audits which utterances/actors actually received a copy (`augmentation_audit` in `results/runs/*.json`, asserted ⊆ training set), and `src.checks` verifies that requesting copies for validation actors fails; (2) each epoch draws **one** version per utterance (the original, or with p=0.8 one of the 4 copies), so an epoch has exactly 960 samples — the training set is **not** expanded 5×, keeping epoch length and early stopping comparable; (3) feature standardisation is fitted on the **original (non-augmented)** training utterances only. | librosa pitch shift / time stretch are too slow to run per sample per epoch (≈99+ runs × up to 100 epochs). | Augmentation diversity is limited to 4 waveform variants × random SpecAugment. | **approved** (Khalid, 2026-11-21) |
